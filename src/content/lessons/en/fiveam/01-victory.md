The book’s Victory Hour divides an hour into three 20-minute pockets: Move through exercise; Reflect through journaling, meditation, prayer, or quiet thought; and Grow through reading or learning. The sequence creates a simple routine, but the exact wake time is optional. Protect sufficient sleep and adapt the hour to your life.
