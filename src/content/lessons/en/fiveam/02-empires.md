The book names four “interior empires”: Mindset (how you think), Heartset (emotional life), Healthset (physical wellbeing), and Soulset (meaning and values). Use them as a broad personal check-in rather than a scorecard or diagnosis. A balanced week can include learning, emotional care, movement and rest, and time connected to what matters.
