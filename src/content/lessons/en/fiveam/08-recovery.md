The book’s Twin Cycles framework contrasts a High Excellence Cycle of focused effort with a Deep Recovery Cycle of rest and replenishment. It also includes the Second Wind Workout and a two-session weekly massage idea among its tactics. Treat recovery as personal: a walk, quiet time, social connection, sleep, or bodywork may suit different people. No single practice is required.
