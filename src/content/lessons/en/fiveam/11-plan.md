Use the frameworks as ingredients: an intentional first hour, a short reflection, focused work on an important outcome, ongoing learning, and real recovery. Choose a wake time that protects sleep. Begin with a version you can repeat, review what helps, and adjust when work, health, family, or seasons change. Consistency matters more than copying someone else’s exact timetable.
