The Tight Bubble of Total Focus means deciding what can enter a chosen work period: silence notifications, prepare materials, and set expectations with others. The 60/10 method pairs a focused work block with a brief recovery break. The specific interval is a suggestion in the book, not a universal productivity law—choose an interval that fits the task and your needs.
