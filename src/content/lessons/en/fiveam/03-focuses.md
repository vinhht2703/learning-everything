The book presents four focuses: Capitalization IQ (use your strengths and opportunities well), Freedom from Distraction (protect attention), Personal Mastery Practice (keep improving through practice), and Day Stacking (make today’s choices support the life you want over time). These are prompts for action, not a demand to be productive every minute.
