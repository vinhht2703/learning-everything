The Weekly Design System is a regular review of outcomes, commitments, focused work, and recovery. Evening preparation can reduce morning friction: set out materials, decide the first task, and make the wake-up plan realistic. If a 5:00 wake time shortens your sleep, shift the wake time or bedtime; the goal is a sustainable routine, not sleep restriction.
