The 60-Minute Student tactic encourages daily learning; Traffic University suggests using suitable travel or routine time for educational material. The point is curiosity and deliberate practice, not filling every spare minute. Use audio only when it is safe and appropriate, such as public transit or hands-free listening without distraction; driving always takes full attention.
