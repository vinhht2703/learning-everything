The book describes habit change through three 22-day stages: deconstruction (interrupt the old pattern), installation (repeat the new routine), and integration (make it part of normal life). It calls this a 66-day minimum. Treat the timeline as the book’s practice framework, not a universal scientific deadline—habit timelines vary. Build a cue, a small action, and an easy way to restart after a missed day.
