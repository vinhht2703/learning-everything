The 90/90/1 rule proposes spending the first 90 minutes of the workday on the single most important project for 90 days. The Daily 5 asks you to define five specific actions that would make today meaningful. These are planning prompts, not rigid quotas: choose a realistic block and fewer actions if that supports better work.
